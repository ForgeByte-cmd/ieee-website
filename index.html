What is IEEE?

IEEE stands for the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers. It is the world's largest professional organisation for people who work with technology.

What it does

IEEE brings together engineers, researchers, teachers and students. It publishes research, organises conferences and writes technical standards, such as the ones used for Wi-Fi.

Why it exists

IEEE's mission is to advance technology for the benefit of humanity. Sharing knowledge, new ideas and research helps everyone build better things.

Why it matters to you

It connects you with a worldwide community of learners and professionals. You can learn from experts, meet people in your field and take part in projects.

What is a Student Branch?

A Student Branch is the IEEE group inside a college. The IEEE VVITU Student Branch runs workshops, competitions and talks, and helps students become part of the wider IEEE community. First-year and second-year students are welcome to take part.
